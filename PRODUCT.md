# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small ecommerce operations teams in Tanger who confirm orders, prepare stock, assign deliveries, track fulfillment, and review profitability from a phone throughout the working day. They need fast, reliable shared updates with enough context to act without opening several tools.

## Product Purpose

Tanger Orders is the operational source of truth for orders, inventory, delivery activity, staff confirmation, and profit. Success means the team can capture an order once, keep its status and costs accurate, plan delivery work, and understand business performance without manual reconciliation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a well-kept paper ledger translated into a responsive operations tool: warm enough for daily use, precise enough for financial decisions.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon finance terminals, decorative analytics, dense enterprise tables on mobile, and unfamiliar controls that slow routine order work. Avoid showing metrics without a clear period, unit, or operational implication.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next operational action within one tap.
2. Keep money and status calculations traceable to real orders and inventory costs.
3. Use progressive disclosure so mobile screens stay focused while secondary tools remain available.
4. Preserve familiar controls, readable labels, and consistent feedback across web and Android.
5. Show comparisons and trends only when they help the team decide what to do next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic labels, touch targets suitable for one-handed phone use, reduced-motion support, and status cues that do not rely on color alone. Support Arabic and English order content without breaking layout.
